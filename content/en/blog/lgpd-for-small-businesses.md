Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) applies to companies of every size. The good news is that compliance can start with simple steps.

## 1. Map the data you collect

List which personal data comes into the company, through which channel and for what purpose.

## 2. Review the legal basis for each processing activity

Not every activity depends on consent. Contracts, legal obligations and legitimate interest are valid bases too.
