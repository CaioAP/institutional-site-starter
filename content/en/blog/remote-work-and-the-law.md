Remote work requires a few specific formalities.

## Written in the contract

The arrangement must be stated in the employment contract or an amendment.

## Equipment and expenses

The contract must say who provides and maintains the equipment.

## Working-hours control

Depending on the arrangement, working-hours control is still mandatory.
