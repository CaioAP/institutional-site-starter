## What data we collect

Data sent through the contact form (name, email, phone and message) and, if you consent, browsing data for statistics.

## What we use it for

To reply to your message and, with your consent, to understand how the website is used.

## Cookies

We use analytics cookies only after you consent. You can change your choice at any time under "Cookie preferences" in the footer.

## Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing contato@exemplo.com.br.
